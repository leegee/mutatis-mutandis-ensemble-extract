# Revelation 1:12–16 — The Son of Man vision

**Textual language:** Greek
**Focus:** Revelation 1:13–14, especially 1:14
**Status:** Early physical witness dossier underway.

## Description in the received Greek text

Revelation 1:14 describes the head and hair as white, compared with white wool and snow. The exact wording and variants should be recorded witness by witness.

A standard printed form is:

`ἡ κεφαλὴ αὐτοῦ καὶ αἱ τρίχες λευκαὶ ὡς ἔριον λευκὸν ὡς χιών`

This is a normalised printed text, not a transcription of P98. The P98 note records the fragmentary papyrus evidence separately.

## Scope and cautions

- The passage describes head and hair.
- It does not explicitly describe the figure's skin colour.
- The comparison with wool and snow invites comparison with Daniel 7:9, but the nature and direction of literary dependence require argument.
- Do not infer that the description necessarily denotes ordinary old age.

## Earliest witness research

- [[P98 - Papyrus 98]] is the current starting point for the earliest physical witness to Revelation 1:14.
- P98 is damaged; the wording is partly reconstructed.
- Its palaeographical date is approximate, not an exact year.
