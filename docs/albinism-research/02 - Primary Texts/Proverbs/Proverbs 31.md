## **Snow — שָׁלֶג (šāleg)**

Snow

**שָׁלֶג**

_šāleg_

Strong's H7950 · Masculine singular noun. This is the ordinary Hebrew word for snow.

## **3. Scarlet — שָׁנִים (šānîm)**

**שָׁנִים**

_šānîm_

Strong's H8144 · Masculine plural noun

This is where things become considerably more interesting.

The word belongs to the Hebrew colour vocabulary associated with scarlet or crimson dye.

It occurs in other biblical passages, often in the expression:

**תּוֹלַעַת שָׁנִי — tôlaʿat šānî**

Literally, something like _worm/scarlet_, conventionally translated as _scarlet yarn_, _scarlet material_ or _crimson cloth_.

The first element, תּוֹלַעַת (_tôlaʿat_), refers to a worm or insect. The colour was obtained from the bodies of scale insects, particularly the kermes insect, which produced a valuable red dye.

This is an important distinction: _šānî_ does not simply mean red in the general sense. It refers to a particular crimson/scarlet dye and, by extension, material coloured with it.
