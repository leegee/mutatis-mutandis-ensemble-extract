# Rígsþula, stanza 34

The text in the Codex Wormianus tradition, in a commonly printed normalized form, is:

**Snorri's Old Norse**

>Svein ól Móðir, silki vafði,
> jósu vatni, Jarl létu heita;
>bleikt var hár, bjartir vangar,
>ǫtul váru augu sem yrmlingi.

**Literal translation**

>Móðir bore a boy, wrapped him in silk,
>sprinkled him with water, and had him called Jarl;
>pale/fair was his hair, bright his cheeks,
>keen/terrible were his eyes, like a young serpent.

Source: [Finnur Jónsson, Rígsþula (1932)](https://www.heimskringla.no/wiki/R%C3%ADgs%C3%BEula_%28FJ%29?utm_source=chatgpt.com); see also the [Old Norse text with English translation](https://www.voluspa.org/rigsthula31-35.htm?utm_source=chatgpt.com).

- Stanza 26 in some older translations. 
- Stanza 33 in Finnur Jónsson's arrangement.
- Stanza 34 in the modern numbering used by several editions.

|Old Norse|Meaning|Significance|
|---|---|---|
|**bleikt**|Pale, fair, yellowish|Describes the hair|
|**bjartir**|Bright, shining, fair|Describes the cheeks|
|**ǫtul**|Fierce, formidable, keen|Describes the eyes|
