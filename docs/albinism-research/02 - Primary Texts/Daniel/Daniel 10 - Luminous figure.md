# Daniel 10:5–6 — The luminous figure

**Textual language:** Biblical Hebrew
**Passage:** Daniel 10:5–6
**Status:** Comparative passage; not direct evidence for white hair.

## Description

The visionary figure is described through several striking comparisons:

- body like beryl;
- face like lightning;
- eyes like flaming torches;
- arms and legs like the gleam of burnished bronze;
- voice like the sound of a multitude.

The passage's visual vocabulary is luminous and metallic. It does not explicitly describe white hair or pale skin.

## Research relevance

This passage may be useful for studying the broader visual language of extraordinary or non-ordinary human appearance. It should not be treated as a direct parallel to Daniel 7's wool-white hair.
