> \[12] And I turnede that I shulde se the vois that spac with me, and I turnede and I saw sevene candilstikis of gold, 
> \[13] and in the middel of the sevene golden candilstikis, oon lyk to the sone of man clothid with a long garnement and gird at the tetis with a golden girdel, 
> \[14] and the heed of hym and hise heeris weren whiyte as whyt wolle and as snow, and the iyen of him as a flawme of fyr, 
> \[15] and hise feet lyk to latoun as in a brennynge chymnei, and the vois of him as the vois of manye watris.
