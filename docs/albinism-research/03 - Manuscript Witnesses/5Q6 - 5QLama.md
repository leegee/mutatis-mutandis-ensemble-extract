# 5Q6 — 5QLama, Lamentations manuscript

## Identification

- **Work:** Lamentations
- **Language:** Hebrew
- **Findspot / inventory:** Cave 5 at Qumran; inventory number 5Q6, conventionally designated 5QLama
- **Date:** Late Herodian, commonly estimated at approximately 30 BCE–68 CE on palaeographic grounds. This is the date of the surviving copy, not the composition date of Lamentations.
- **Relevant coverage:** Fragment 1, column i, lines 3–5 are assigned to Lamentations 4:7; the surrounding fragment covers 4:5–8.

## Textual evidence for Lamentations 4:7

The Qumran Digital transcription associates the opening of the verse with line 3:

> זכו נזיריה

Its line 4 transcription is heavily fragmentary and uses brackets for restoration:

> [משלג צחו מח]לב [א]ד֯מו עצם מפ֯נינ֯[ים ספיר]

The line 5 transcription preserves part of the final colon:

> [גזרתם

The exact bracket and uncertainty conventions must be checked against the digital transcription and, ultimately, the plate in the DJD edition. The crucial limitation for the present dossier is that משלג (“than snow”) and צחו are supplied in brackets in the displayed line, while only the end of חלב is visible outside them. Therefore 5Q6 is an early physical witness to the verse, but it does not independently preserve the complete snow comparison.
