# Geʿez 1 Enoch tradition

**Language:** Geʿez (Classical Ethiopic)
**Work:** 1 Enoch
**Status:** A textual tradition, not a single manuscript witness.

## General position

The Geʿez version is the oldest complete surviving form of 1 Enoch. This does not make it the earliest physical witness: Aramaic Qumran fragments and Greek papyri are earlier surviving witnesses to portions of the work.

The dates of the translation, individual manuscripts, and surviving codices must be distinguished.

## Noah description

Prior discussion noted the Geʿez form `ጸዐዳ` (*ṣəʿəda*; exact vocalisation/transliteration to verify) in the colour description, and raised the question of why this form appears rather than an expected form associated with the Semitic root *l-b-n*.

Do not assume the answer. Possible explanations to investigate include lexical convention, translation equivalence, Vorlage, and the semantics of the relevant Geʿez root.
