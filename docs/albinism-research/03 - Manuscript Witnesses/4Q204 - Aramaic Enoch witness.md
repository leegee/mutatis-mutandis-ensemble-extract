# 4Q204 — Aramaic Enoch witness

**Collection:** Dead Sea Scrolls / Qumran
**Work:** 1 Enoch
**Language:** Aramaic
**Relevant passage:** 1 Enoch 106:2 (reported; verify fragment and line assignment)
**Date:** Commonly placed in the late Second Temple period; exact palaeographical range to be checked against the edition/catalogue.
**Status:** Core witness; details need edition-level verification.

## What is reported as preserved

Prior research records the form `שמוק` (*šmwq*), interpreted as “red” or “reddish”, in the Noah description.

## What is not preserved

The surviving Aramaic fragment does not, on the evidence currently recorded here, preserve an Aramaic word for “white” in this description.

This negative fact is methodologically important. The Greek expressions “whiter than snow” and “whiter than white wool” must not be projected backwards into the Aramaic witness as though they were legible there.
