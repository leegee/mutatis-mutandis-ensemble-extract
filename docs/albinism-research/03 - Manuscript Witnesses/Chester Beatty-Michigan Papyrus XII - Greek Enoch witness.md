# Chester Beatty–Michigan Papyrus XII — Greek Enoch witness

**Work:** 1 Enoch
**Language:** Greek
**Date:** Reported as fourth century CE; confirm catalogue/edition dating.
**Coverage:** Reported to preserve 1 Enoch 97:6–107:3, with chapter 105 absent; includes 106:1–107:3.
**Status:** Important Greek physical witness; transcription and catalogue data to verify.

## Relevant reading: 1 Enoch 106:2

Reported Greek phrases:

- `λευκότερον χιόνος` — “whiter than snow”
- `πυρρότερον ῥόδου` — “redder than a rose”
- `τρίχωμα ... λευκότερον ἐρίων λευκῶν` — hair/locks ... “whiter than white wool”

The exact text, morphology, lineation, and lacunae must be checked against the papyrus edition.

## Editorial reconstruction

The clause represented as:

`⟨οὐχ ὅμοιος ἀνθρώποις καὶ τὸ χρῶμα αὐτοῦ⟩`

has been identified in prior discussion as editorially restored rather than securely legible on the papyrus. It must always be marked as supplied by an editor when quoted.

## Research significance

This witness supplies Greek wording that is not preserved in the surviving Aramaic fragment at the corresponding point. The difference is evidence for the Greek textual form, not automatic proof of what the Aramaic Vorlage said.
