In _De excidio et conquestu Britanniae_, probably written in the sixth century, Gildas quotes Lamentations 4:7:

> _candidiores nazaraei eius niue, rubicundiores ebore antique, sapphiro pulchriores._

English by Hugh Williams:

>Nazarenes were whiter than snow, ruddier than old coral, fairer than sapphire.

Machine Translation:

> "Her Nazirites were whiter than snow, redder than ancient ivory, more beautiful than sapphire."

He introduces this as part of Jeremiah's lament over Jerusalem. He then applies the lament to the Church, identifying the sons of Zion with _sancta mater ecclesia_, the holy mother Church.
