## The Pani (Medieval Bestiary)

> The Pandi, also called Pandae, were a legendary race described in medieval bestiaries and Greco-Roman geographical writings. They were imagined as a people with very large ears, eight fingers and toes, and **white hair that darkened with age**. The historian Ctesias placed them in the mountains of India, portraying them as a warlike folk of archers and spearmen, and claimed they were born with a full set of teeth. Pliny the Elder added that they lived for two hundred years. The name refers to a category of fabulous people rather than a single individual. In the modern novel The Burning Maze, they appear as loyal servants of the emperor Caligula.
