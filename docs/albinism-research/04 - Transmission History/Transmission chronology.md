## Dating vocabulary

Always specify whether a date refers to:
- composition of a literary unit;
- redaction or final form;
- translation into another language;
- production of a surviving manuscript;
- palaeographical estimate;
- later copying or reception.

## Open chronology problems

- Establish the date and textual history of the Aramaic Enochic source.
- Identify the Greek translation's date independently of the Chester Beatty papyrus.
- Identify the date of the Geʿez translation separately from individual Geʿez codices.
- Verify the Latin Royal manuscript's date and exact textual content.
- Add a fuller Revelation witness stemma only when supported by textual evidence.
