# Glossary

- **Composition date:** Approximate date when a literary work or unit was composed.
- **Redaction:** Editorial shaping or combination of earlier material into a textual form.
- **Vorlage:** The source text presumed to underlie a translation or textual witness.
- **Physical witness:** An extant manuscript, papyrus, inscription, or other artefact preserving text.
- **Diplomatic transcription:** A transcription intended to represent the manuscript's actual written forms and features.
- **Editorial supplement:** Text supplied by an editor where the manuscript is damaged, missing, or illegible.
- **Palaeographical dating:** Dating based on the forms and development of handwriting.
- **Reception history:** The history of how a text is read, interpreted, translated, and reused.
- **Geʿez:** Classical Ethiopic, the language of the complete surviving Ethiopic version of 1 Enoch.
- **Lacuna:** A gap in the surviving text.
