## Working conclusions — provisional

1. Daniel 7:9 describes the Ancient of Days with clothing white like snow and head-hair like clean/white wool. The precise force of the Aramaic wool adjective requires lexical and textual scrutiny.
2. Daniel 10:5–6 presents a luminous, metal-like visionary figure; it is not a straightforward parallel for white hair or pale complexion.
3. In 1 Enoch 106:2, the Greek witness contains comparisons involving whiteness and redness. The surviving Aramaic fragment is reported to preserve a word meaning “red”, but does **not** preserve an Aramaic word for “white”. Do not back-translate the Greek wording as though the Aramaic were extant.
4. P98 is an early physical witness to Revelation 1:14, but the relevant line is damaged and partly reconstructed. Its dating is palaeographical and approximate.
5. These passages may be compared as a history of visual description, but literary dependence or a single continuous tradition must not be assumed without evidence.
