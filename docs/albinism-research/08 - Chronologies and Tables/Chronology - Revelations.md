### **Transmission and reception**

1. **Greek Revelation**
    
    1st-century composition; surviving Greek witnesses include P98 (c.200–250) and Sinaiticus (4th century).
    
2. **Early Latin Revelation**
    
    In circulation by the late 2nd/early 3rd century; Tertullian and Cyprian provide early evidence.
    
3. **Old Latin manuscript**
    
    Fleury Palimpsest, 5th century; physically preserves Revelation 1:14–15.
    
4. **Vulgate revision**
    
    A distinct Latin textual form, represented by Fuldensis (541–546).
    
5. **Insular transmission**
    
    Bede, c.703; Amiatinus, c.700–716; early Irish and Insular commentary traditions.
