# Salters - Lamentations (ICC)

## Full citation

Salters, R. B. *Lamentations: A Critical and Exegetical Commentary*. International Critical Commentary. London: T&T Clark, 2010.

## Relevant pages

- Lamentations 4:7-8, printed pp. 301-303 in the accessible edition. Confirm page numbering against the copy used for formal citation.

## Argument / findings relevant to this dossier

- Salters reads the first half of verse 7 as physical brightness/whiteness rather than moral purity, partly because צחו ("dazzling/bright") stands in parallel and the poem proceeds to colour verbs for red and dark.
- He takes אדמו as "be red/ruddy." He considers עצם an adverbial accusative meaning "in body" possible, while acknowledging that this rendering has been regarded as forced.
- He notes that the ancient versions differ in how they handle עצם and the final sapphire phrase, reflecting genuine syntactic and lexical difficulty.
- He reads verse 7's colour imagery against the reversal in verse 8, while treating the details of the second half of verse 7 as uncertain.
