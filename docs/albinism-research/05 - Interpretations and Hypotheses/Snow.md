### **Hebrew**

Snow is repeatedly used in the Hebrew Bible as a reference point for extraordinary whiteness.

Some particularly interesting examples:

- **Exodus 4:6** — Moses' hand becomes white as snow when afflicted with leprosy (צָרַעַת, _ṣāraʿat_).
    
- **Numbers 12:10** — Miriam becomes white as snow when afflicted with _ṣāraʿat_.
    
- **2 Kings 5:27** — Gehazi becomes white as snow.
    
- **Psalm 51:7** — The speaker asks to be washed and become whiter than snow.
    
- **Isaiah 1:18** — Sins, though scarlet, shall become white as snow.
    
- **Daniel 7:9** — The Ancient of Days has clothing white as snow.
    
- **Daniel 7:9** also describes his hair as like pure wool.
