Jerome's commentary (Book II, Daniel 7:9) contains several identifiable scriptural connections.

|Element|Jerome's supporting text|Interpretation|
|---|---|---|
|Ancient of Days|Daniel 7:9|Eternal judge|
|Heavenly throne|Revelation 4:2 onwards|Correspondence between Daniel and John's vision|
|White clothing|Matthew 17|Christ's transfiguration and divine majesty|
|Hair like pure wool|No source explicitly cited|Impartiality and uprightness of judgement|
|Fiery throne|Deuteronomy 4:24|God as consuming fire|
|Burning wheels|Ezekiel 1|God's fiery chariot|
|Fire proceeding from throne|Psalm 96:3|Divine judgement|

Notice the gap: Jerome supplies biblical parallels for almost every element, but does not cite an earlier authority for the interpretation of the hair.

His explanation is:

> The even-handedness and uprightness of His judgment is shown forth, a judgment which shows no partiality in its exercise.

This is Jerome's own explicit explanation in the surviving text. We should not yet attribute it to Hippolytus, Origen or a Jewish source.
