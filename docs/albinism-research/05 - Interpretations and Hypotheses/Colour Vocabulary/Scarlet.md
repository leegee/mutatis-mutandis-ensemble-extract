# Hebrew

Three useful comparisons:

| Passage          | Hebrew context  | Significance                                             |
| ---------------- | --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Genesis 38:28–30 | שָׁנִי (_šānî_) | Scarlet thread placed around Zerah's hand                |
| 2 Samuel 1:24    | שָׁנִי (_šānî_) | Scarlet clothing associated with the daughters of Israel |
| Isaiah 1:18      | שָׁנִי (_šānî_) | Sins compared to scarlet                                 |
| [[Proverbs 31 - red-white]]  |                 |                                                          |

Isaiah 1:18 is especially worth noting:

> Though your sins be as scarlet, they shall be as white as snow.

The Hebrew makes the contrast particularly striking:

- שָׁנִי — scarlet
    
- שֶׁלֶג — snow
